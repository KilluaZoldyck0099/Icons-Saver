# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with people, especially people who are new
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainers by opening a confidential issue or contacting them directly. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
